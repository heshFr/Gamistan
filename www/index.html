HOW TO PLAY

  • 🐾 Your cat climbs forever. Drag or use ← → to steer through the gaps.
  • 💀 When you die, you respawn — but your last run becomes a numbered ghost cat replaying the same course. Don't touch your past selves!
  • 🐟 Grab fish for points & combos. Golden fish are worth 10×.
  • ⚡ Snag power-ups: shields, magnets, rockets, catnip frenzy & more.
  • 🐉 Climb through 8 biomes and beat the nine-tailed guardians at each peak.
  • 🛍️ Spend fish on cats, hats, trails & permanent upgrades.
